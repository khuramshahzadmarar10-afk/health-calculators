Common questions

Are these SehatCalc accurate?

They use established general formulas, but every result is an estimate. Individual needs can differ because of health conditions, body composition, medications, pregnancy, breastfeeding and other factors.

Is BMI suitable for everyone?

BMI is a population-level screening tool, not a diagnosis. It may be less informative for children, older adults, pregnant people and people with high muscularity; a clinician can interpret it in context.

Does water intake include tea, coffee and food?

Yes. Total fluid intake can include water from other drinks and moisture-rich food. Your environment, exercise intensity and health can change how much you need.

Where should I get Sehri and Iftar times?

Use a trusted local mosque, recognised prayer timetable or reliable authority for your location. Enter those times here; this page does not guess your location or fetch prayer data.

Is any personal information saved?

Tool inputs and calculation results stay in this browser tab and are not saved or uploaded by this page. Google Analytics does send usage data, such as page visits and device information, to help measure site traffic. Closing or refreshing the page clears the results.

About Our Free SehatCalc

SehatCalc brings four useful online wellness tools together in one clear, mobile-friendly place. The free BMI calculator uses adult height and weight to estimate body mass index and show a standard weight category. BMI can be a helpful starting point for understanding weight in relation to height, but it does not directly measure muscle, bone, or body fat. For that reason, a BMI result should not be treated as a diagnosis or as a replacement for a clinical assessment or a dedicated body fat calculator.

The daily calorie calculator estimates maintenance energy needs with the Mifflin–St Jeor equation and an activity factor. Enter age, sex, height, weight and activity level to receive an estimated number of calories per day, plus gentle weight-loss and weight-gain reference figures. The water intake calculator creates a daily hydration estimate from body weight, exercise time and climate. Results are displayed in litres, millilitres and approximate cups, making the hydration calculator easy to use for everyday planning. Individual calorie and fluid needs can vary, so use every estimate as general information rather than personalised medical or nutrition advice.

The Sehri and Iftar fasting timer helps you plan a daily fast using times from your trusted local mosque, prayer timetable or recognised authority. Enter the local Sehri end time and Iftar time to see a live fasting countdown and planned duration on your device. This tool does not automatically fetch prayer times or location data. All four online SehatCalc work without an account, and the values entered into the tools are not saved by this page. Whether you are checking BMI, estimating daily calorie needs, planning water intake or following a fasting schedule, these simple calculators are designed to make useful health numbers easier to understand. Always speak with a qualified healthcare professional about symptoms, medical conditions, medication, pregnancy, breastfeeding, major diet changes or fasting safety.
